📅 Tuesday – Lord Hanuman
Mantra:

Hanuman Beej Mantra

Om Namo Hanumate Namah

Chalisa: Hanuman Chalisa

Aarti: Aarti Kije Hanuman Lala Ki

Katha: Mangalvar Vrat Katha

Stotra: Bajrang Baan, Hanuman Ashtak

Vrat: Mangalvar Vrat for strength & courage

Extras: Importance of Sindoor & Laddoo

📅 Wednesday – Lord Krishna / Lord Ganesha (regional variations)
Mantra:

Om Gan Ganapataye Namah

Hare Krishna Hare Rama

Chalisa: Ganesh Chalisa / Krishna Chalisa

Aarti: Jai Ganesh Jai Ganesh Deva / Om Jai Jagdish Hare

Katha: Budhwar Vrat Katha (for wisdom and clarity)

Stotra: Ganesh Atharvashirsha / Gopal Sahasranam

Vrat: Budhwar Vrat (for intellect, business)

Extras: Offer Durva grass, bananas

📅 Thursday – Lord Vishnu / Sai Baba / Brihaspati Dev
Mantra:

Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya

Om Sai Namo Namah

Chalisa: Vishnu Chalisa / Sai Chalisa

Aarti: Om Jai Jagdish Hare / Aarti Sai Baba

Katha: Brihaspativar Vrat Katha (for prosperity & wisdom)

Stotra: Vishnu Sahasranama / Sai Baba Ashtotram

Vrat: Peela (yellow) food offerings, fast with chana daal

Extras: Importance of banana tree worship, yellow clothes

📅 Friday – Goddess Lakshmi / Santoshi Maa / Durga
Mantra:

Om Shreem Mahalakshmiyei Namah

Jai Santoshi Maa

Chalisa: Lakshmi Chalisa / Durga Chalisa

Aarti: Jai Ambe Gauri / Om Jai Lakshmi Mata

Katha: Santoshi Mata Vrat Katha

Stotra: Kanakdhara Stotra / Durga Saptshati excerpts

Vrat: Fast with gur-chana, no sour food

Extras: Decorate with red flowers, light 16 diya

📅 Saturday – Lord Shani / Lord Hanuman (again) / Kal Bhairav
Mantra:

Om Sham Shanicharaya Namah

Om Namo Hanumate Namah

Chalisa: Shani Chalisa / Hanuman Chalisa

Katha: Shanivar Vrat Katha

Stotra: Shani Stotra by Dashrath / Hanuman Ashtak

📅 Sunday – Lord Surya / Lord Vishnu
Mantra:

Om Suryaya Namah

Aditya Hridaya Stotra

Chalisa: Surya Chalisa

Aarti: Om Jai Surya Dev

Katha: Ravivar Vrat Katha

Stotra: Aditya Hridayam
